FAQ / Help Center

Quick answers to common questions about the Z28 Attendance Bot.

What is the Z28 Attendance Bot? +

Z28 helps groups manage attendance activities and monitor current activity from the Telegram Mini App.

Who can access the group dashboard? +

Your Telegram account must be a group owner or administrator, and the bot must still be available in that group.

How do I change activity limits? +

Open the group dashboard, choose Edit on the relevant settings card, update the values, then press Save. You need the required group permissions.

What does Auto Refresh do? +

When enabled, the dashboard checks for updated group activity every 30 seconds. It is off by default and can be turned on from the dashboard header.

Why can’t I see a group? +

Make sure the bot is still in the group and that your Telegram account is a group owner or administrator. Groups that are no longer available to the bot are not shown.

How do I report a problem? +

Open Help & Support, choose a category, describe the problem, and send the report. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.
